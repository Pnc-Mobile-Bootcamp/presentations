Requirements:

S	- specific
M	- measurable
A	- achievable
R	- relevant
T	- testable


The app should be fast - not measurable, not testable, not specific
- The timesheet list screen must render within 1.5 seconds at P95 on a 4G LTE connection


The app should be secure - not specific
- All authentication tokens must be stored in the device Keychain (iOS) or Keystore (Android)
	and must expire after 8 hours of inactivity

Users should be able to manage their profiles - not specific - what are the acceptance criteria?
- Users must be able to update their display name, email address, and notification preferences
	from the profile screen

Metrics for Scrum:

Velocity - story points completed per sprint
		- forecast sprint capacity
		- DO NOT use to compare teams

Burndown Chart
	- remaining (uncompleted) work (in story points) plotted against time (remaining in sprint)

Burnup Chart
	- work completed against total scope
	- problem: how do we know the total # points for the entire project?

Cycle Time
	- time from when a story is started until it is Done
	- long cycle time indicates we have too much work-in-progress
		"Stop starting and start finishing"

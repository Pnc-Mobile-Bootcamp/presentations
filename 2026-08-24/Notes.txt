The app should be fast - not measurable, not testable, not specific
- The timesheet list screen must render within 1.5 seconds at P95 on a 4G LTE connection


The app should be secure - not specific
- All authentication tokens must be stored in the device Keychain (iOS) or Keystore (Android)
	and must expire after 8 hours of inactivity

Users should be able to manage their profiles - not specific - what are the acceptance criteria?
- Users must be able to update their display name, email address, and notification preferences
	from the profile screen
